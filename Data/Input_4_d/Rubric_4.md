# SWEN3204 Assignment 2 – Marking Rubric

Total: 40 marks. Award whole marks only. Each criterion is marked within the mark range of the level that best describes the answer.

| Criterion | Marks | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1 Quality attributes** | 6 | **5–6 marks.** Two attributes that clearly matter most are chosen and justified from the scenario. Both scenarios have all six parts and both response measures are measurable. | **3–4 marks.** Suitable attributes are chosen with some justification. Scenarios are mostly complete but one is missing a part or has a response measure that is not measurable. | **1–2 marks.** Attributes are named with little or no justification, or the scenarios are not in six-part form or are not measurable. | **0 marks.** Not attempted or irrelevant. |
| **Q2 Architectural style** | 6 | **5–6 marks.** A clear recommendation justified by at least two trade-offs that are specific to the scenario (team size, load profile, known problems). The downside of the recommendation is acknowledged. | **3–4 marks.** A clear recommendation with two trade-offs, but one is generic or not linked to the scenario, or no downside is acknowledged. | **1–2 marks.** A recommendation with only one trade-off, or with generic textbook advantages and disadvantages only. | **0 marks.** Not attempted or no recommendation. |
| **Q3 Availability calculation** | 8 | **7–8 marks.** Parts (a), (b) and (c) are all correct with working shown, to the requested precision. | **5–6 marks.** The method is correct throughout but there is one arithmetic or rounding slip, or exactly one part is incorrect. | **1–4 marks.** The series calculation is attempted but two or more parts are incorrect, or working is not shown. | **0 marks.** Not attempted or no correct element. |
| **Q4 Resilience pattern** | 5 | **5 marks.** An appropriate pattern is named, its mechanism is explained correctly, it is applied to the outage, and at least one drawback or tuning consideration is given. | **3–4 marks.** An appropriate pattern is named and explained correctly, but it is only loosely applied to the outage or no drawback or tuning consideration is given. | **1–2 marks.** A pattern is named but the explanation is incorrect or very thin. | **0 marks.** Not attempted or an unsuitable pattern. |
| **Q5 Consistency and availability** | 6 | **5–6 marks.** Both choices are justified, the CAP theorem is used correctly, and a suitable concrete technique is named for each case. | **3–4 marks.** Both choices are reasonable and techniques are named, but the CAP theorem is explained incorrectly or imprecisely, or one technique is missing. | **1–2 marks.** Choices are stated with little justification, or the CAP theorem is not used. | **0 marks.** Not attempted. |
| **Q6 Architecture decision record** | 5 | **5 marks.** Context, Decision and Consequences are all present and specific to the scenario, with at least one positive and one negative consequence. | **3–4 marks.** All three sections are present but the consequences are one-sided (no negative consequence) or the context is generic. | **1–2 marks.** One or more sections are missing or the record does not describe a decision. | **0 marks.** Not attempted. |
| **Communication and format** | 4 | **4 marks.** Every answer is within 150 words, terminology is precise, the required headings are used and sources are cited in IEEE style. | **3 marks.** Clear writing, but one answer exceeds the word limit or there are minor citation or formatting problems. | **1–2 marks.** Several answers exceed the word limit, or the writing is hard to follow, or sources are not cited. | **0 marks.** Format requirements ignored. |
