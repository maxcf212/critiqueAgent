# Input_2_u - what was changed (NOT sent to any model)

UPGRADED ("_u") variant of `Input_2`. `Rubric_2.md`, `Task_2.md` are byte-identical copies of the originals; only the solution differs (`Solution_2_u.md`, derived from `Input_2/Solution_2.md`).

The report was rewritten section by section to address every reason for lost marks. The student's mission, satellite name, orbit (600 km sun-synchronous, 97.79 deg), ground station, five-year life, drag-sail disposal, the five STK images and all fifteen STK access values are kept. New design details (50 kg mass, 5 m / 60 km imager, data rates, 4 m2 sail) and all hand calculations were added for this dataset; the calculations were computed and checked and are correct. References are real publications written from the author's knowledge and were not re-checked online. One thing could not be verified: the RAAN of 27.33 deg is the student's STK value and is kept as given; the stated node local time (about 08:45, descending) was inferred from the access times in the student's table, and a marker who recomputes the node local time from RAAN and epoch may find the two do not agree.

**Basis for the edits:** consolidated marking of Input_2 from 37 CritiqueAgentv3 gradings (18 runs in `runs/compare_20261112-074138_Input_2_md`, 18 in `runs/compare_20261112-082532_Input_2_md`, 1 reference run). Original marks: mean 61/100, range 49-78; per criterion C1 7.7/10, C2 37.6/60, C3 6.2/10, C4 9.7/20. The 'Why' column gives how many of the 37 runs raised each point.

**Expected effect on marking:** All four criteria should reach Accomplished (roughly 85-95%). Known remaining gaps, stated in the report itself: no link budget, and the STK files are listed in an appendix but cannot be attached to a markdown dataset.
