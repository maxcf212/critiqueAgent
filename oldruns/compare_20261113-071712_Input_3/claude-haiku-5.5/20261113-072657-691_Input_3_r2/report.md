## Technical issues

- DICUSSION: To Ship or Not to Ship: Apple/Samsung/Google competitive claim is supported only by a CNBC marketing article that does not address rivals; the citation does not substantiate the argument.
- The Most Ethical Path: Volkswagen case involved deliberate defeat devices (intentional fraud), which is a weak analogy for shipping known defects under time pressure; the distinction is not discussed.
- The Most Ethical Path: EACE clause 2.3b (outside competence) is misapplied to the QA timeframe; the clause concerns qualifications and experience, not schedule constraints. Clause references should be verified against the code.
- The Most Ethical Path: Does not address the scenario's distinction between physical-harm failures (never shipped) and data-loss failures (shipped), nor a practical rule for when Rachel may ship known defects.
- References: Reference formats are inconsistent and incomplete: BBC entry lacks URL; NYT entry has garbled author names and a broken access date; the word count may exceed the 1,500 limit.
