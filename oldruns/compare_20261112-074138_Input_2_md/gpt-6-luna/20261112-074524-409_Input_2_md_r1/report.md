## Technical issues

- Orbit: A sun-synchronous orbit provides approximately constant local solar time at a given latitude; it does not guarantee continuous sunlight. The report's claims of a constant sunlight supply and that the descending path is constantly in sunlight are not generally valid.
- Orbit: The reported RAAN value does not indicate the launch facility location; RAAN specifies the orientation of the orbital plane relative to the reference direction. Launch-site access depends on launch azimuth, launch window, and achievable inclination.
- Orbit: The report says the orbit is circular and that argument of perigee and true anomaly are undefined, but then lists argument of latitude as 0° without specifying an epoch or initial state. A complete initial orbit state needs a defined epoch and a consistent set of elements.
- Launch: The proposed Vega E launch is presented as a likely 2025 option, although its development and schedule were uncertain; the report itself notes that the vehicle had not been built. A rideshare to a 600 km, 97.79° orbit is not demonstrated to be available.
- Ground Control & Communications: The access table includes apparent duplicate access entries and inconsistent date/time ordering, so the stated frequency and mean contact duration should be checked against the STK scenario and report interval.
- Ground Control & Communications: The claimed high pass frequency from a more polar ground station is not a sufficient basis for selecting a site. Access depends on station latitude, orbit geometry, elevation mask, and the chosen analysis period; no elevation mask or link budget is given.
- Mission Duration & EOL: A drag sail can increase atmospheric drag, but the report gives no sail area, mass, ballistic coefficient, deployment reliability, or orbit-decay estimate to show that the spacecraft will deorbit within an acceptable timeframe.
- Orbit: The report identifies 600 km altitude and orbital inclination but does not substantiate the imaging design with ground sample distance, sensor performance, swath, or revisit analysis; the stated whole-reef daily coverage is not demonstrated.
