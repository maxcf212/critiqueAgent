## Technical issues

- Orbit: A sun-synchronous orbit needs RAAN to precess about 360° per year (about 0.9856°/day eastward), which the text states as 1° per day and mixes with a full rotation per year; it also says RAAN is 'the initial value indicates the location of the launch facility', which is not how RAAN is defined.
- Orbit: Argument of latitude is described as the angle from ascending node, but the table gives u=0° while stating it changes with position, an internal inconsistency; the Keplerian element table lists only five elements, omitting true anomaly/mean anomaly.
- Mission Design - Launch: Launch from Kourou at 5.05°N is said to be used for a retrograde near-polar orbit with a 'piggyback' ride, yet the text then selects a dedicated Vega E; the claim that Kourou's latitude below target inclination gives two windows per day is only loosely justified.
- Ground Control & Communications: Claims a polar ground station maximises pass frequency and then selects Tasmania (about 42°S) without quantifying the trade-off; the 'verticality' claim about contact duration is unsupported.
- Mission Duration & EOL: Drag sail paragraph is truncated mid-sentence; the uncontrolled descent option is said to be chosen but then rejected for lack of thrust without a re-entry or lifetime calculation.
- Orbit: Altitude of 600 km was chosen by swath argument but the swath width and ground resolution are never calculated; the reef width of 250 km is not related to sensor swath numerically.
