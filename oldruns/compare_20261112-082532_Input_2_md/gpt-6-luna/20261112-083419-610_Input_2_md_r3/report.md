## Technical issues

- Orbit: The report describes the descending ground-track path as constantly sunlit and suggests a constant supply of sunlight. A sun-synchronous orbit does not guarantee continuous sunlight; eclipse conditions depend on orbital geometry and season. No local time of ascending node is specified to establish the imaging illumination conditions.
- Orbit: The statement that the initial RAAN indicates the location of the launch facility is incorrect. RAAN describes the orientation of the orbital plane at an epoch; it is not the launch-site location. The initial orbital state also lacks an explicit epoch and reference frame.
- Orbit: The mission does not quantify sensor ground resolution, spectral capability, imaging swath, or the ability to detect bleaching through water. The 600 km altitude selection therefore is not demonstrated to meet the proposed fluid-lensing and reef-imaging needs.
- Launch: The Vega E choice is prospective and has no demonstrated rideshare availability, payload mass compatibility, or confirmed mission matching. The report does not establish that a suitable launch opportunity would inject the spacecraft into the stated orbit.
- Ground Control & Communications: Mount Pleasant is identified as a radio observatory, but the report does not establish its suitability as a spacecraft TT&C ground station. No communications band, antenna/link budget, data rate, or ground-control capability is specified.
- Ground Control & Communications: The access table displays a duplicated 603.382-second record, while the stated total duration and mean correspond to only 15 records rather than all displayed rows. This makes the reported access statistics ambiguous.
- Mission Duration & EOL: The drag sail is proposed without a device size, deployment reliability basis, or estimated orbital decay and re-entry time. The report therefore does not demonstrate that the EOL plan will achieve disposal within an appropriate timeframe.
- Bibliography: The report cites Wikipedia as a source, which the assignment explicitly does not accept as a proper source. Several in-text/table reference numbers also do not appear in the bibliography.
