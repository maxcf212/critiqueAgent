- Orbit: The 600 km altitude and 97.79° inclination are plausible for a sun-synchronous orbit, but no sensor, spectral bands, ground sample distance, signal-to-noise analysis, or water-depth capability is given to establish that fluid-lensing reef imagery is feasible.

- Orbit: RAAN specifies the orientation of the orbital plane, not the launch facility's location. The initial RAAN and argument of latitude lack an epoch or specified local time of the ascending node, so the initial orbit geometry is not fully defined.
