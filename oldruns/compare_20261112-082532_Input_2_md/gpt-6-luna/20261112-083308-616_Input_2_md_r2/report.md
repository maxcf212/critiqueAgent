## Technical issues

- Orbit: The report says RAAN indicates the location of the launch facility. RAAN specifies the inertial orientation of the ascending node; it is not determined by launch-site location alone, and the stated value is not justified by a launch-time or orbit-plane analysis.
- Orbit: A sun-synchronous orbit does not inherently provide continuous sunlight to the spacecraft. Eclipse exposure depends on the orbit's local time of node and seasonal geometry; the report does not specify these or establish continuous illumination.
- Orbit: The report selects 600 km altitude and discusses a 250 km reef width but supplies no sensor field of view, swath, ground resolution, spectral capability, or target-access analysis to show that fluid-lensing observations are feasible or that useful reef coverage is achieved.
- Ground Control & Communications: The access table repeats the 5 February 13:00 access as entries 14 and 15. This duplicate may inflate the stated access count and contributes to the reported statistics.
- Mission Duration & EOL: The drag-sail disposal concept has no sail sizing, decay-time estimate, or compliance demonstration. A passive drag sail is not an orbital manoeuvre, so the report's description of EOL as the mission's orbital manoeuvre is inconsistent.
- Launch: Vega E is described as unbuilt and its proposed schedule is speculative, yet it is selected without demonstrating a confirmed launch opportunity, rideshare availability, or delivery to the required orbit.
