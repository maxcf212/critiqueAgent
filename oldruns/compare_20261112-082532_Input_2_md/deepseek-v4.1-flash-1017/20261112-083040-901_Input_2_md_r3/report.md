- Launch: Reasoning that Kourou's 5.05 deg latitude gives two daily launch windows at ascending/descending node is incorrect; no discussion of the performance penalty of a 97.8 deg retrograde injection from Kourou.
- Ground Control & Communications: Access table duplicates access 14/15 and has inconsistent numbering; no explicit passes-per-day or data-volume/link budget; Mt Pleasant is a radio telescope, suitability for TT&C/downlink not demonstrated.
- Orbit vs EOL: Earlier states the only manoeuvre is at EOL, later states the satellite has no thrust capability; EOL drag-sail sentence is truncated with no altitude/lifetime trade.
- Mission Duration & EOL: Suggests solar panels could 'lengthen' life, but power generation is a baseline requirement, not an optional lifetime extension.
- Overall: No mass, power, data-rate or sensor specification; orbit selection not tied to actual instrument coverage requirement.
