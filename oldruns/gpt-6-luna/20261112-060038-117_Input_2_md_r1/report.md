## Technical issues

- Orbit: The listed RAAN is said to indicate the launch facility's location. RAAN is the inertial orientation of the orbital plane and depends on launch timing and injection geometry, not launch location alone; an epoch is also needed to make the element set reproducible.
- Orbit: No local time of ascending or descending node is specified. Therefore the claimed consistent illumination and the assertion that descending passes are in sunlight cannot be established; SSO does not by itself make a particular pass direction continuously sunlit.
- Orbit: The proposed 600 km altitude is not supported by payload ground-resolution, swath, or imaging-performance calculations. The report also does not quantify how the imager would cover the reef or meet its monitoring needs.
- Launch: The proposed Vega E launch is described as a future, not-yet-built vehicle, and no confirmed launch, rideshare availability, payload interface, or injection compatibility is established.
- Ground Control & Communications: Mount Pleasant is selected because of its latitude, but the report does not establish that its radio-telescope facilities provide compatible spacecraft TT&C, frequencies, tracking, or operational access. No link budget or data-rate analysis substantiates the claim that contact time is sufficient.
- Ground Control & Communications: The access table contains a duplicated access entry (entries 14 and 15) and the stated access results do not demonstrate data delivery or communication capacity; access duration alone is insufficient evidence.
- Mission Duration & EOL: The report proposes a drag sail but gives no device sizing, deployment design, lifetime analysis, or disposal-time estimate. It also calls the descent passive while discussing an orbital manoeuvre, despite stating the spacecraft has no thrusters.
- Introduction: The bibliography cites Wikipedia for the spaceflight calendar, contrary to the task's requirement not to use Wikipedia as a proper source; references are otherwise largely bare URLs without full bibliographic details.
