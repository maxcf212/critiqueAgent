## Technical issues

- Orbit: The reported 600 km, 97.79° near-circular orbit is broadly plausible for a sun-synchronous orbit, but a specific local solar time of the ascending/descending node is not given. Therefore the assertion that the descending track is constantly sunlit and the claimed illumination conditions are not established.
- Orbit: RAAN is not determined by the launch facility's location. The statement that the initial RAAN indicates the launch facility is incorrect; injection geometry, launch epoch, and the selected orbital plane determine the initial RAAN.
- Launch: The report selects Vega E while acknowledging it had not been built. This is not a demonstrated available launch option; rideshare eligibility, deployment orbit compatibility, schedule, and capability are not substantiated.
- Orbit: The report gives no sensor specification, ground sample distance, spectral capability, swath, or underwater-performance analysis to show that a satellite at 600 km can provide useful fluid-lensing reef imagery or the stated monitoring products.
- Ground Control & Communications: The access table contains a duplicated access entry and reports a maximum contact of about 12.8 minutes, not an average of almost ten minutes. It does not establish data volume or link-budget adequacy.
- Ground Control & Communications: A near-polar orbit does not by itself ensure long contact for the vast majority of passes; contact depends on station latitude, elevation mask, orbit geometry, and ground-station antenna constraints.
- Mission Duration & EOL: The report describes an EOL deorbit manoeuvre but also states the satellite has no thrust capability. A drag sail is a passive disposal device, not an orbital manoeuvre; no deployment design or decay-time estimate is provided.
- Mission Duration & EOL: A five-year useful lifespan is asserted without supporting spacecraft reliability, power, or consumables analysis. The text also suggests solar panels may lengthen life without explaining the limiting-life mechanism.
- Bibliography: Reference [6] is Wikipedia, which the assignment explicitly disallows as a proper source. Citations numbered [49]–[74] in the launch tables do not have corresponding bibliography entries.
